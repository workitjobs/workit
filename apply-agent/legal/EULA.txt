4. You submit each application

application: you check each one and click Submit yourself. Sign-ins, the codes that job
sites send you, captchas and other bot checks are also yours to do, and the app never
tries to get around them. You are responsible for what you submit, and for following the
rules of each job site, hiring system and employer. AI writes some of the answers, and AI
can be wrong: read every answer before you submit it.
